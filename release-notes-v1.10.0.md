**Download [TrayVitals-Setup.exe](https://github.com/Fakem1d/TrayVitals/releases/download/v1.10.0/TrayVitals-Setup.exe)** below, under Assets, and run it. Updating from an older version keeps your settings. If you already have 1.9.0, TrayVitals offers this update by itself.

## New in 1.10.0
- **TraySense is now TrayVitals.** Same app, a name of its own so it isn't mixed up with the original TraySense by DMTWEAKS. Updating moves your settings, shortcuts and startup choice over and removes the old TraySense files. TraySense 1.9.0 finds this update by itself (it's also attached as TraySense-Setup.exe for that).
- **New look.** A redesigned menu with switches and buttons that stays open while you change settings, plus new popups and a new What's new window.
- **Two menu styles.** Keep the modern menu or use Windows' standard one. Pick one in setup, or switch any time from the menu (Menu style).
- **Themes.** Follow Windows (light or dark), or pick Light, Dark, Midnight, Graphite, Forest or Sakura.
- **Transparency.** Make the menu and popups see-through, and set how see-through the taskbar labels are.
- **GPU power on AMD Radeon cards.** It's new, so if the number looks wrong on your card, please open an issue and say which card you have.
- **New setup.** Four short steps (Welcome, Menu style, Options, Install), with Back and Continue.
- **License.** TrayVitals stays free to use, change and share, but from this version on it may not be sold or used commercially. The original code by DMTWEAKS keeps its MIT License; see [LICENSE](https://github.com/Fakem1d/TrayVitals/blob/main/LICENSE).
